# 🌪️ DIASTRA — Disaster Intelligence & Alert System

> **An intelligent, real-time disaster monitoring and early warning platform for India.**

DIASTRA is a disaster management platform designed to bring **weather information, disaster alerts, risk analysis, and geographic visualization** together in one place.

The platform aims to help users understand **where disasters are occurring, what risks are developing, and which areas may require attention**.

---

## 🚨 Why DIASTRA?

India is vulnerable to several natural disasters, including:

* 🌊 Floods
* ⛰️ Landslides
* 🌀 Cyclones
* 🌧️ Heavy Rainfall
* ⚡ Thunderstorms & Lightning
* 🌡️ Heat Waves
* 🌍 Earthquakes
* 🌾 Droughts

Disaster-related information is often distributed across different sources and agencies.

**DIASTRA brings this information together into a unified platform for easier monitoring and understanding.**

---

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Disaster Map

Visualize disaster events, warnings, and risk zones on an interactive map of India.

### 🌧️ Weather Monitoring

Monitor important environmental and weather conditions to understand developing risks.

### 🚨 Disaster Alerts

Display relevant disaster warnings and alerts from reliable official sources.

### 🌊 Flood Risk Monitoring

Analyze relevant environmental information to identify areas that may require increased flood-risk monitoring.

### ⛰️ Landslide Risk Monitoring

Use geographical and environmental information to highlight areas that may be vulnerable to landslides.

### 📊 Risk Classification

DIASTRA presents risk levels in a simple and understandable format:

| Level | Status   |
| ----- | -------- |
| 🟢    | Low      |
| 🟡    | Moderate |
| 🟠    | High     |
| 🔴    | Severe   |

### 📍 Location-Based Information

Users can select a location and view relevant weather conditions, disaster alerts, and risk information.

## 💡 How DIASTRA Works

DIASTRA follows a simple workflow:

**1. Data Collection**

Relevant weather, environmental, and disaster information is collected from available data sources.

**2. Data Processing**

The collected information is processed and organized by the backend.

**3. Risk Analysis**

Environmental and disaster information is analyzed to identify potential risk levels.

**4. Visualization**

The processed information is displayed through an interactive dashboard and map.

**5. Early Warning**

Users can quickly identify developing hazards and areas requiring attention.
